Chapter 1: The Missing Light

Mira found the little lighthouse dark on the night of the village festival. At its door, her friend Finn held up an empty lantern.

"The ships need that light. Where did the lantern go?" Mira asked.

"I saw a glow near the old garden," Finn replied. "Let's look together."

They followed a trail of golden sparks through the grass.

Chapter 2: A Light to Share

In the garden, a tiny fox curled beside the missing lantern. Its warm light had kept her safe from the cold.

"You needed it too, didn't you?" Mira said softly.

"We can bring a blanket and leave her a smaller light," Finn suggested.

Together they made the fox a cozy bed, then carried the lantern back to the lighthouse. Its beam swept across the harbor, and the festival boats answered with a chorus of cheerful bells.
